Pepe's Italian & Liquor

Pepe's Italian is your go to date-night spot. Italian can be agreed upon that it is the middle ground when it comes to most favourable cusines, and Pepe's perfects the small but simple menu that they have. The food is as good as it gets, but the best part about Pepe's is the beautiful interior AND exterior that they offer. The interior is a dimmly lit room providing more of a romantic vibe whilst the exterior is adorned with neo light bulbs, Italian statues and a fresh garden.

  • Cuisine type: Italian
  • Signature dishes: Paccheri Alla Bolognese ($38) & Caeasar Salad ($26)
  • Deposit Amount for Reservation: $60
  • Price range per person: $40-$60

Ippudo Ramen

Ippudo is a very well known Ramen restaurant within the heart of the CBD, located at QV. This place is ideal for people to really exprience Japanese culture not only through food but the atamosphere as well. Everytime you enter you are greeted by all staff who shout "Irasshaimase" which indirectly translates to "Welcome" or "Please come in!". It perfectly represents the culture you'd normally expect at your every day restaurant in Japan. Foodwise, as the name suggests, Ippudo specialises in Ramen and has been known to constantly do justice to its Japanese authenticity.

  • Cuisine type: Japanese
  • Signature dishes: Shiromaru ($30) & Chicken Karaage Curry ($25)
  • Deposit Amount for Reservation: $40
  • Price range per person: $20-$40

Pochana

Pochana is a standard, "lets have a feast!" type Thai restuarant that you can NEVER go wrong with. This one is a great family friendly dine in exprience, with a beautiful cozy interior exprience and an even more vibrant menu that is surpsingly visually appealing (great for kids). With a warm and friendly staff and generous portions it is no wonder that this place is very popular. However we advise to book in advance as the resturant can exprience long que times becuase of this!

  • Cuisine type: Thai
  • Signature dishes: Phuket, Fried Rice ($32) & Beef Fat Fried Rice ($26)
  • Deposit Amount for Reservation: $20
  • Price range per person: $20-$40

Operator Diner

The Operator Diner is a classic mix between American and Italian food. Where Pepe's solely focus downpat your fancy Italian dishes, the diner inspires a constrast of good and filling food that has an occasional fancy italian dish here and there. It's a great brunch spot to stop along the way with some mates and continue on some other activities within Melbourne afterwards, whilst also having the flexiblity for a more casual date night as well.

  • Cuisine type: Italian/American
  • Signature dishes: Be Ham-ble ($16) & Main Chick ($22)
  • Deposit Amount for Reservation: $40
  • Price range per person: $20-$40

MCKINLY Eatery

Looking for a more relaxed lunch? A quick catch up with a friend? or just a casual and relaxing stop? Well look no further than MCKINLY Eatery! It's most likely the best brunch spot on the list as it's a hybrid between a good coffee pick up and luxrious brunch spot.

  • Cuisine type: Breakfast/Brunch
  • Signature dishes: Belly Benny ($28) & Cheesesteak Toastie ($23)
  • Deposit Amount for Reservation: $20
  • Price range per person: $20-$40

Hakoya Izakaya

Hakoya is a great place for a group of friends to stumble upon duirng a late night hangout. Another Japanese resturant that accommidates to the Japanese culture, they offer a large variety of group shared platters as well big portion foods. It's an all-round good restuarant if you want to go home on full stomach. Service as always is exceptionally nice and as well as offer a great range of Japanese alcohol, which is why this one is mainly recommend for adults!

  • Cuisine type: Japanese
  • Signature dishes: Tonkastu ($22) & Teriyaki Salmon Steak ($25)
  • Deposit Amount for Reservation: None
  • Price range per person: $20-$40
